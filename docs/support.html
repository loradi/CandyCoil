Support

Need help with Candy Coil? Email ldiego900@gmail.com and include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue. Please do not send your Google password or verification codes.

Gameplay

Candy Coil can be played solo without signing in. Use the on-screen touch controls to steer. A run ends when your Coil hits another snake or the arena boundary. Crossing your own body is allowed.

Online features

Google sign-in, player profiles, friend codes, and rooms are experimental. Live synchronized multiplayer is not available yet.
